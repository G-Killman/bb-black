The idea behind Bayou Bytes

How This Happened

It started with a simple frustration.

I got tired of scrolling through social media trying to find a tattoo I'd seen the day before. It was gone — buried under newer posts and nearly impossible to find again.

Then I realized it wasn't just tattoo shops. Hair salons, nail technicians, photographers, artists — any business that depends on showcasing its work faces the same problem. Great work gets lost in the endless scroll.

That's why I created a gallery-focused website that's clean, organized, and easy to browse. Your work stays online 24/7, customers can find exactly what they're looking for, and reaching out is just a click away.

Best of all, you can have a professional online presence for less than the cost of most people's monthly cable TV bill.

This Is How It Works

If you're interested, contact me for your free website build period. You tell me the colors and theme, then send me the images you want used. I build the site, and you check it out. If you like it, you rent it. If you like it but want to make some changes, we edit it before it goes live.

Once you decide to move forward, we'll figure out which rental plan is right for you. I'll secure the domain name and get you online as soon as your payment clears. Within five business days, you'll be indexed on Google and live for people to find and see your work.

If you decide it's not for you, there is no obligation.
