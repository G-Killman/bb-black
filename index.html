The idea behind Bayou Bytes

How This Happened

It began with a simple observation.

Many businesses rely on social media to showcase their work, but their strongest projects often get buried and become difficult to find.

That is why I built a refined, gallery-focused website solution for tattoo studios, salons, photographers, artists, contractors, and other visual businesses.

The result is a clean, organized online presence that keeps your work visible, accessible, and easy for customers to explore.

All of it at a price that stays far below what most people pay for a monthly cable bill.

How It Works

If you are interested, contact us to begin the initial website build phase. You will provide your preferred colors, branding direction, and the images you would like featured. We will develop the site, and once the preview is ready, you can review it and request revisions before launch.

After you decide to move forward, we will determine the subscription plan that best fits your needs. We will secure your domain and prepare your site for launch as soon as payment is received. Within ten business days, your website will be live and submitted for indexing so customers can begin finding your business online.

If you decide not to proceed, there is no obligation.
